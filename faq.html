Mortgage Assistance Questions, Answered

The answers below are written to be honest about what mortgage assistance can and cannot do. Every situation is different, and the final decision on any request rests with your mortgage servicer and/or investor. If your question is not covered here, please contact our team or read about our services.

What is a mortgage modification?

A mortgage modification is a change to one or more terms of your existing mortgage loan, made to address affordability or an existing delinquency. It is not a new loan and it is not direct financial assistance. A modification is only put in place when your mortgage servicer and, in many cases, the investor who owns the loan agree to the change, and any change is subject to their approval and to the requirements that apply to your loan.

Can a mortgage modification reduce my monthly payment?

It may, depending on the terms of your loan and the options that are available to you. A modification can change the interest rate, extend the loan term, or change how the past-due amount is treated, any of which may lower a monthly payment. However, a reduction is not guaranteed, and some modifications leave the payment unchanged or even increase it. No outcome should be assumed until your servicer or investor has issued a decision.

Can I apply if I am several months behind on my mortgage?

Homeowners at different levels of delinquency may have options available to them, including repayment plans, forbearance and modification. Whether you qualify depends on your loan, your servicer and investor requirements, and your individual financial circumstances. Being behind does not automatically rule out assistance, but it also does not guarantee that a particular option will be offered.

Can I get assistance if my home is already in foreclosure?

Some loss mitigation options may remain available after the foreclosure process has begun, and deadlines in the process matter a great deal. If you have received a foreclosure notice or a scheduled foreclosure sale date, you should contact your mortgage servicer immediately and seek advice from a qualified foreclosure attorney licensed in your state without delay. MMCV cannot guarantee that a foreclosure will be stopped, delayed or prevented. See our Foreclosure Assistance Information for more detail.

Does every homeowner qualify?

No. Approval is not automatic, and it is not available to everyone. It depends on your individual circumstances and on the applicable requirements of your mortgage servicer and the investor who owns your loan. Different loans and different servicers apply different rules, so a result that applies to one homeowner does not necessarily apply to another.

What documents may be required?

Exact requirements vary by servicer, but commonly requested documents include:

  • A recent mortgage statement.
  • Recent pay statements or other proof of income.
  • Bank statements, when the servicer asks for them.
  • A written hardship letter explaining your circumstances.
  • Household expense information.
  • Any notices you have received from your servicer, and any other documents the servicer requests.
How long does the mortgage modification process take?

There is no fixed timeline. Review periods vary and depend on the servicer, the investor, how complete your documentation is, and the complexity of your case. Providing accurate and complete information as early as possible can help avoid unnecessary delays, but it does not control the servicer's schedule. We do not promise any specific timeframe for a decision.

Will my mortgage company approve the modification?

That decision is not ours to make. The mortgage servicer and/or the investor who owns your loan makes the final decision, based on their own requirements and on your circumstances. Submitting an application or working with MMCV does not guarantee approval.

Can you communicate with my mortgage company?

Communication about account-specific information may require your written authorisation. Many servicers will not discuss an account with a third party until they have received a valid written authorisation signed by the homeowner, so we normally need that in place before any account-specific conversation can take place. You may also always contact your servicer directly and independently — you do not need MMCV, or any other company, to speak with your own servicer.

What if I have already received a modification?

Additional assistance after a previous modification may depend on the type and date of the earlier assistance, the investor's rules and your servicer's current requirements. Some homeowners may be considered again after a set period or once their circumstances change, while others may not be eligible for further assistance. We can help you understand which rules may apply, but eligibility remains a decision for your servicer and/or investor.

What if I cannot afford my current mortgage payment?

If your payment is no longer affordable, the most useful thing you can do is provide accurate information about your income and household expenses so that the options available to you can be evaluated properly. Servicers base their review on your current financial picture, so out-of-date or incomplete figures can lead to a poor match or a delay. See our services and how it works for how this information is used.

Do you work with all types of mortgages?

The assistance available depends on the type of loan you have and on the servicing and investor guidelines that apply to it. There is no single set of options that applies to every mortgage, and we do not claim that every loan type is eligible for every programme. During the initial review we help identify which rules are likely to apply to your situation.

Does submitting an application guarantee approval?

No. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval, does not guarantee that your monthly payment will be reduced, and does not guarantee that a foreclosure will be stopped or delayed. An application simply begins a review that your mortgage servicer and/or investor will decide under their own requirements.

What should I do if my foreclosure sale is approaching?

Contact your mortgage servicer immediately and seek advice from a qualified foreclosure attorney licensed in the relevant state as soon as possible. Do not wait for an online application or a company response before taking action. Deadlines in the foreclosure process can be short, and acting early is the best way to understand what options may still be open to you. MMCV cannot guarantee that a foreclosure will be stopped or delayed.
