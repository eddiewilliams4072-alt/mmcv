Disclaimer and Important Disclosures

This page explains the limits of the information MMCV provides and sets out the important disclosures that apply to homeowners.

Effective date: [Effective date to be configured]

1. No Guarantee of Approval

Submitting an application, or receiving assistance from MMCV, does not guarantee that your mortgage servicer or investor will approve a mortgage modification or any other loss mitigation option.

Approval depends on the servicer's and investor's own requirements and on your individual circumstances, including your income, your expenses, the reason for your hardship, and the current status of your loan.

2. No Guarantee of Foreclosure Prevention

MMCV cannot guarantee that foreclosure proceedings will be stopped, delayed or prevented.

Only your mortgage servicer, the investor and the courts have the authority to affect a foreclosure. Timelines and requirements vary, and you should treat any foreclosure deadline as a legal deadline.

3. Servicer and Investor Decisions

All mortgage modification and loss mitigation decisions are made by the applicable mortgage servicer and/or investor according to their own requirements.

MMCV has no authority to make, change or overturn those decisions. Our role is to help you understand the process and to organise the information a servicer may request.

4. Not a Mortgage Lender

MMCV is not a mortgage lender, mortgage broker or loan originator. It does not make, fund, refinance or guarantee mortgage loans.

MMCV does not provide grants or other direct financial assistance, and it does not approve or deny any loan, modification or assistance programme.

5. No Legal or Tax Advice

The information on this website is general information only. It does not replace advice from a licensed attorney, a licensed tax professional, or a qualified financial professional.

You should obtain independent advice about your own circumstances before making any decision about your mortgage, your tax position, or any legal matter, including any pending foreclosure.

6. Important Foreclosure Notice

If you have received a foreclosure notice or have a scheduled foreclosure sale, contact your mortgage servicer immediately and consult a qualified foreclosure attorney in your state. Do not rely on this website or an online application to protect your legal rights or stop a foreclosure.

If you are facing foreclosure, our Foreclosure Assistance page explains the general loss mitigation options that may be available. Information on that page is general and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified foreclosure attorney in your state.

7. No Government Affiliation

MMCV is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any government agency, including any federal, state or local housing agency or programme.

MMCV does not approve or deny any government assistance, and it does not provide government grants or government funds.

8. No Implied Endorsement

No statement on this website should be read as an endorsement, partnership or approval by any mortgage servicer, investor, government agency, or housing counselling organisation.

Any reference to a programme, option or servicer is provided for general information and does not imply any relationship or endorsement.
