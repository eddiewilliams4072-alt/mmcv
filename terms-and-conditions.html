3. Description of Services

MMCV provides guidance to homeowners who are experiencing mortgage difficulties. Our services may include explaining the mortgage assistance process, reviewing the information a homeowner provides, helping to organise documentation, and supporting communication with a mortgage servicer where the homeowner has given written authorisation.

MMCV is not a mortgage lender, mortgage broker or loan originator. It does not make, fund, refinance or guarantee mortgage loans, and it does not provide grants or other direct financial assistance.

The exact services to be provided will be described to the homeowner directly, and no service is provided until both parties have agreed to it.
