Prepare Ahead

Before You Contact Us

A few minutes of preparation can make your conversation with our team more productive.

Have your most recent mortgage statement

Your statement shows your loan number, the servicer's name and contact details, and the amount that is currently past due. Having it nearby helps our team understand your situation.

Note the date of your last payment

Knowing when you last paid, and how many payments have been missed, helps clarify how far along the delinquency is and which questions to ask your servicer.

Write down any notices you have received

Keep a note of every letter or notice from your servicer, including the date it arrived and any deadline it mentions, so that nothing important is overlooked.

Received a foreclosure notice?

If you have received a foreclosure notice or have a scheduled foreclosure sale, contact your mortgage servicer immediately and seek advice from a qualified foreclosure attorney in your state. Please read our foreclosure assistance information for general guidance about what to do next.
