How It Works

A Clear, Four-Step Process

The same structured approach is followed with every homeowner so that nothing is left to guesswork. You may stop at any point, and there is never an obligation to proceed.

  1. Submit Your Information

    Complete the eligibility form with basic details about your mortgage and household finances. The more accurately you complete it, the more useful the initial review will be.

  2. Review Your Mortgage Situation

    Our team reviews the information you provided to understand your circumstances and identify what additional information may be needed. We explain what we can and cannot help with.

  3. Explore Available Options

    Based on your situation, we outline the assistance routes that may be worth discussing with your servicer, including modification, repayment plans and other loss mitigation options.

  4. Understand the Next Steps

    We help you understand what happens next, what documents may be required, and how the servicer review typically works so you can move forward with confidence.

Foreclosure Concerns

Facing Foreclosure? Explore Your Options

If you are concerned about foreclosure, it is important to understand what assistance options may still be available and what steps you can take. The earlier you act, the more options may be open to you.

MMCV provides general information to help homeowners understand the foreclosure process and the loss mitigation options that a servicer may offer. We do not stop or delay foreclosure proceedings, and we cannot guarantee any outcome.

  • Understand the foreclosure timeline in your state
  • Learn about loss mitigation options your servicer may offer
  • Know what documents to gather and when to act
  • Understand when to seek advice from a qualified attorney
