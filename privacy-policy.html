1. Information We Collect

MMCV collects information in a few different ways. What we collect depends on how you use this website and what you choose to share with us. You do not need to create an account to use the website.

The information we collect includes:

  • Information you submit through the forms on this website, including the eligibility and application form on the homepage and the general enquiry form on the contact page.
  • Information collected automatically when you visit the website, such as your browser type, your device type, an approximate location derived from your IP address, the pages you visit, the referring URL, and the dates and times of your visits.
  • Information we receive from third parties where we are permitted to do so, for example where a mortgage servicer provides information to us after you have given written authorisation.

2. Information You Provide

You provide information when you complete the eligibility and application form on the homepage or the general enquiry form on the contact page. Depending on what you choose to enter, this information may include your name, email address, phone number, state of residence, mortgage servicer name, mortgage payment and balance ranges, income and hardship information, and supporting documents that you choose to provide.

The general contact form is not intended to collect sensitive information. Please do not include your Social Security number, full bank account numbers, passwords, or other unnecessary sensitive information in the general contact form or in any free-text field.

You choose what to provide. If you would prefer not to provide a particular item, contact us and we will explain what is needed to assess your request.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry and to evaluate your request for assistance.
  • To communicate with you about your enquiry.
  • To contact you by phone, email or text message about your enquiry.
  • To prepare and organise documentation.
  • To communicate with a mortgage servicer where you have given us written authorisation to act on your behalf.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the website.
  • To comply with our legal obligations.

Where you ask us to contact you by phone, email or text, you consent to that contact for the purpose of your enquiry. You may ask us to stop contacting you by a particular method at any time.

4. How Information May Be Shared

We may share the information you provide in the limited circumstances described below:

  • With service providers that help us operate the business, such as website hosting, email delivery, analytics, and customer relationship management providers.
  • With a mortgage servicer or investor, where you have authorised us to communicate on your behalf.
  • With our professional advisers, such as lawyers and accountants, where this is necessary.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or other transfer of the business or its assets.
  • Where we are required to do so by law, or where disclosure is necessary to protect our legal rights or the rights of others.

The categories of information shared, and the parties with whom it is shared, depend on the service you have asked us to provide.

This website relies on third-party providers to function. Form and email delivery on this website is handled by EmailJS, and the typography used on the website is loaded from Google Fonts. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms, which govern their handling of that information.

We may also use web analytics to understand how the website is used. Analytics providers may set their own cookies and process data under their own policies.

Other third-party providers: [Third-party service providers to be listed]. Links to third-party websites are governed by those websites' own terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures designed to protect the information we hold against unauthorised access, use, loss or disclosure.

No method of transmission over the internet, and no method of electronic storage, is completely secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of information, and you provide information to us at your own risk.

If we become aware of a security incident affecting your personal information, we will take appropriate steps and provide notifications as required by applicable law.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry, to provide any services you have requested, to meet our recordkeeping and legal requirements, and to resolve disputes.

Our specific retention period is: [Data retention period to be configured].

When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete it or to remove the details that identify you.

8. Cookies and Analytics

This website and its third-party providers may use cookies and similar technologies. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help the website to function and help us to understand how the website is used.

Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their settings, and you can opt out of certain analytics. If you block cookies, some features of the website may not work as intended.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the state in which you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information. These rights can include the right to access, correct or delete your information, the right to obtain a copy of it, and the right to opt out of certain sharing or targeted advertising.

To make a request, contact us using the email address on this page or through the contact page. We may need to verify your identity before we can act on a request, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

10. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for adults. MMCV does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or, for the purposes of applicable state law, under 16.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date shown at the top of this page.

If we make material changes, we will post the updated policy on this page.
