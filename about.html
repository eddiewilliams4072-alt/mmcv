About MMCV

Who We Are

MMCV provides mortgage assistance guidance to homeowners who are experiencing financial difficulties, who have missed mortgage payments, or who are concerned about foreclosure. We work with homeowners throughout the United States who want to understand what options may exist for their mortgage situation.

We are not a mortgage lender and we do not provide loans, grants or direct financial assistance. Our role is to help homeowners understand their situation, organise the relevant financial information, and communicate with their mortgage servicer where we are properly authorised to do so.

Every homeowner's circumstances are different. The options that may be available depend on the loan type, the investor's guidelines, the homeowner's financial position and how far behind the mortgage is. Our work begins with listening carefully and explaining the process honestly.

What we help with

  • Understanding a mortgage account and what may have caused the delinquency.
  • Reviewing mortgage and financial information.
  • Explaining potential mortgage assistance and loss mitigation options.
  • Organising the documentation a servicer may require.
  • Supporting communication with mortgage servicers when authorised.

Please note: MMCV does not decide the outcome of a mortgage assistance request. Mortgage modification, repayment plan and loss mitigation decisions are made by the applicable mortgage servicer and/or investor according to their own requirements. Approval is never guaranteed.

What We Focus On

Four Areas of Mortgage Assistance Guidance

Our work is centred on helping homeowners understand their position and take well-informed next steps.

Mortgage Assistance Guidance

We help homeowners understand their mortgage situation and the range of assistance routes that may apply to their loan and servicer.

Mortgage Modification Support

We explain how a modification application is typically prepared and what servicers commonly require before they will review a request.

Financial Documentation Assistance

We help homeowners organise income, expense and hardship documentation so that the information submitted is complete and consistent.

Foreclosure Prevention Options

We explain the loss mitigation options that may still be available when a mortgage is in default, and the deadlines that matter.

MMCV cannot promise that any homeowner will qualify for a mortgage modification or avoid foreclosure. Eligibility and outcomes depend on the homeowner's circumstances and on the requirements of the mortgage servicer and investor.

Our Approach

A Structured, Transparent Way of Working

We follow the same five steps with every homeowner so that nothing is left to guesswork.

  1. Understand your situation

    We start by understanding what has happened with the mortgage, when payments were missed, and what has changed in your household finances.

  2. Review your mortgage and financial information

    We review the details you provide, together with any mortgage statement you are able to share, to build an accurate picture of the account.

  3. Explain potential assistance options

    We explain the options that may be worth exploring with your servicer, including what each option generally involves and what it does not do.

  4. Help organise the next steps

    We help you identify which documents and figures are needed, and in what order, so that your information is complete and up to date.

  5. Support communication and documentation

    Where you have given us the appropriate written authorisation, we may assist with correspondence and coordination with your mortgage servicer. You are always free to contact your servicer directly and independently.
